Design a closed-die drop-forged and CNC-finished Ti-6Al-4V Titanium Motorsport I-Beam Engine Connecting Rod assembly in millimeters lying flat on the build plate:

1. **Crankshaft Big-End Journal Ring & Split Bearing Cap**:
   - 58 mm crankpin bore inner diameter (78 mm outer diameter, 24 mm thickness) centered at one end of the rod, complete with a precision bronze/babbitt split bearing shell liner and twin bolt shoulders on the left and right flanks.
   - Two high-tensile 12-point ARP cap fasteners threaded into the big-end shoulders clamping the lower cap to the rod beam.

2. **Tapered H-Beam / I-Beam Shank with Web Pocket**:
   - A high-strength forged beam connecting the big-end crank journal to the small-end wrist pin eye across a 135 mm center-to-center rod length.
   - Raised 24 mm tall outer I-beam flanges framing a recessed 10 mm thick central web pocket to minimize reciprocating mass while resisting buckling under peak combustion pressure.
   - Material: forged Ti-6Al-4V titanium.

3. **Piston Wrist-Pin Small-End Eye & Pressed Bronze Bushing**:
   - 24 mm inner diameter (38 mm outer diameter, 24 mm thickness) wrist-pin boss at the opposite end with a pressed phosphor-bronze sleeve bushing and top oiling hole.

Make key dimensions adjustable via live sliders (center-to-center length, crank bore diameter, wrist pin bore diameter, rod thickness, and beam width).
