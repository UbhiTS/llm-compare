1. **Precision-Ground Base Thermal Spreader Plate**:
   - 120 mm long by 90 mm wide by 8 mm thick solid thermal baseplate sitting flat on the ground plane with 4 counterbored corner mounting slots.
   - Material: high-conductivity C18150 chromium-zirconium copper.

2. **3D-Printed Self-Supporting Diamond/Truss Lattice Core**:
   - A multi-layer 3D open-cell structural pin-and-cross-strut lattice matrix rising 44 mm above the baseplate (printable at 45-degree overhang angles without internal support removal), maximizing surface-area-to-volume heat transfer.
   - Material: laser-sintered titanium / AlSi10Mg metallic finish.

3. **Upper Conformal Collector Manifold & Threaded Inlet/Outlet Nozzles**:
   - An upper arched collector plenum header capping the lattice core, featuring two vertical flanged and externally threaded fluid port nozzles (26 mm outer diameter, 16 mm inner bore) for high-flow liquid or two-phase cooling.
