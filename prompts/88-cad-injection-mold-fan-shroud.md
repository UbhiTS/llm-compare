Design an injection-molded glass-filled nylon (PA66-GF30) Automotive Cooling Fan Shroud & Stator Ring assembly in millimeters resting flat on the build plate:

1. **Rectangular Radiator Mounting Plenum Deck**:
   - 150 mm by 150 mm rectangular outer shroud frame (12 mm skirt height, 3.0 mm nominal wall thickness) with perimeter sealing ribs and 4 corner mounting tabs with brass compression-limiter bushings.
   - Material: matte charcoal glass-filled nylon plastic.

2. **Converging Venturi Bellmouth & Circular Fan Ring**:
   - A smooth converging nozzle ring rising 28 mm from the rectangular plenum deck around a 112 mm inner diameter fan swept throat to guide airflow without recirculation.

3. **Radial Stator Support Struts & Central Brushless Motor Hub**:
   - A 54 mm diameter central cup-shaped motor mount boss suspended at the center of the fan ring by 6 curved aerodynamic stator guide vanes (2.5 mm rib thickness) that double as structural ribs and airflow straighteners.
   - Four snap-fit wire-harness retention clips around the outer shroud perimeter.
   - Material: dark molded thermoplastic shroud with a brushed aluminum central BLDC motor rotor bell.

Make key dimensions adjustable via live sliders (plenum size, fan throat diameter, shroud height, stator vane count, and wall thickness).
