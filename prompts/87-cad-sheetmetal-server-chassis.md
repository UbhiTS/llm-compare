Design a stamped and press-brake formed Sheet-Metal 1U Rackmount Server Module Carrier Tray in millimeters resting flat on the build plate:

1. **Main Sheet-Metal U-Channel Pan & Bent Side Walls**:
   - 160 mm wide by 120 mm deep base pan formed from 2.5 mm thick cold-rolled steel / aluminum sheet, with left and right 90-degree vertical side walls rising 42 mm (1U rack envelope) and inward-bent top stiffening return flanges.
   - Front left and right EIA-310 rackmount ears extending outward by 18 mm on each side with slotted rack screw cutouts and anodised front pull handles.
   - Material: brushed galvalume / stainless sheet steel with dark anodized rack handles.

2. **Punched Airflow Louvers & Bottom Weight-Reduction Cutouts**:
   - Stamped ventilation slots along the floor pan and rear exhaust wall for front-to-back server airflow.

3. **Pressed Self-Clinching PEM Standoffs & Hot-Swap Drive Divider**:
   - Six pressed brass self-clinching M3 hex/cylindrical standoffs (8 mm tall) arranged on the tray floor to support a server motherboard, plus a central sheet-metal partition wall dividing dual hot-swap NVMe drive bays.

Make key dimensions adjustable via live sliders (tray width, tray depth, wall height, sheet thickness, and standoff height).
