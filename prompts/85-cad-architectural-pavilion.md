Design a scale model of a free-form Timber and Glass Diagrid Observatory Pavilion in millimeters (overall footprint roughly 200 mm diameter by 85 mm height) resting flat on the ground plane:

1. **Stepped Circular Stone Plinth**:
   - A 206 mm outer diameter, 8 mm tall stepped circular podium base with a recessed central courtyard well.
   - Material: light architectural precast concrete / matte ceramic stone.

2. **Twisted Diagrid Glazing Canopy**:
   - A self-supporting toroidal/hyperboloid shell rising 74 mm above the plinth from a 95 mm base radius up to a 44 mm diameter open sky oculus ring at the crown.
   - 12 helical structural ribs spiraling around the translucent architectural glass envelope with horizontal tension rings at 4 elevation tiers.
   - Material: frosted sky-blue laminated architectural glass with structural glulam ribs.

3. **Central Helical Observation Staircase**:
   - A spiral timber staircase winding upward around the central core column from the ground plinth toward the upper observation ring.
   - Material: warm oak glulam timber treads and central column.

Make key dimensions adjustable via live sliders (span radius, apex height, diagrid rib count, ring tiers, and oculus radius).
