Act as the Chief Global Logistics Officer and Principal Intermodal Operations Researcher for an international freight forwarding conglomerate managing trans-Pacific maritime, rail intermodal, and long-haul drayage networks (140,000 Twenty-Foot Equivalent Units / TEUs annually). A compounding disruption has struck the primary trans-Pacific trade corridor: an unannounced labor slowdown at the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach combined with severe freight rail congestion at the Chicago intermodal interchange has paralyzed container flows.

Container dwell times at port marine terminals have exploded from 3.2 days to 16.4 days, trans-load warehouses are at 100% capacity, accumulated container demurrage and detention liabilities exceed $400,000 daily, chassis shortages prevent container clearance, and $550M in seasonal retail and industrial components face missing critical contractual delivery windows.

### Deliverables & Section Requirements:

#### 1. Bottleneck Impact Assessment & Financial Liability Model
- Quantitative impact baseline table categorizing stranded container volume across 5 priority tiers (Perishable Cold-Chain, Promotional Retail Goods, Just-in-Time Automotive Components, Consumer Electronics, Low-Margin Raw Materials).
- Table columns: Priority Tier, Stranded TEU Count, Cargo Value ($), Daily Accruing Demurrage ($285/day) & Rail Detention ($190/day), Days Until Complete Production/Shelf Stockout, and Contractual Non-Delivery SLA Penalties.
- Total accumulated 30-day financial liability exposure under status-quo operations.

#### 2. Comprehensive Intermodal Rerouting Decision Matrix
Construct an exhaustive trade-off evaluation table comparing 4 alternative routing bypass strategies against the congested baseline:
- **Option 1: Status Quo (Ride Out Congestion)**: LA/Long Beach dwell + BNSF/UP rail intermodal to Chicago.
- **Option 2: Pacific Northwest (PNW) Diversion**: Rerouting maritime vessels to Tacoma/Seattle or Prince Rupert (CN Rail) for direct rail bypass into the Midwest.
- **Option 3: All-Water Panama Canal Routing**: Vessel transit through Panama Canal directly to US East/Gulf Coast ports (Houston, Savannah, Norfolk).
- **Option 4: Mexico Landbridge (Lázaro Cárdenas)**: Vessel discharge at Lázaro Cárdenas, Mexico, utilizing CPKC cross-border international rail into Texas and the Midwest.
- **Option 5: Emergency Air Freight & Expedited Team Drayage**: Selective air charters for critical JIT components + cross-country non-stop team-driver drayage.
- Table columns: Transit Time Delta (days), Total Landed Freight Cost Delta per TEU ($), Carrier Reliability Score (%), Customs/Border Crossing Friction, and Max Weekly Capacity (TEUs).

#### 3. Off-Dock Container Drayage & "Inland Port" Staging Architecture
- Operational architecture for the "Pushover / Peel-Off" container clearance model:
  - Establishing 24/7 dedicated drayage block moves to off-dock inland rail yards and dry port container depots in the Inland Empire (Ontario/Victorville, CA).
  - Off-terminal block-stacking protocols by final zip-code destination, eliminating individual container hunting at congested marine terminals.
- Chassis pool balancing model: private chassis cooperative fleet deployment and dual-transaction turnaround optimization.

#### 4. Class I Railroad Intermodal Scheduling & Interchange Bypass
- Rail operating plan to bypass the congested Chicago rail interchange bottleneck:
  - Blocking unit trains to bypass Chicago classification yards directly to secondary intermodal hubs (Kansas City, Columbus, Indianapolis).
  - Steel-wheel versus rubber-tire cross-town interchange trade-off analysis.
  - Rail car equipment availability and well-car supply chain positioning.

#### 5. Landed Cost & Product Margin Sensitivity Table
Comprehensive financial sensitivity table modeling the landed cost and profit margin impact on 6 representative cargo commodities under each rerouting scenario:
- Columns: Commodity, Base Wholesale Margin (%), Landed Cost Increase ($/unit), New Net Margin (%), Inventory Carrying Cost Increase ($), and Net Profitability Recommendation (Reroute vs. Air Expedite vs. Cancel Order).

#### 6. Executive Contingency Protocol & Carrier SLA Negotiations
- Carrier contractual force majeure assessment and demurrage dispute runbook under Federal Maritime Commission (FMC) Ocean Shipping Reform Act (OSRA) guidelines.
- Customer communication transparency portal specification providing real-time GPS container visibility, revised delivery ETAs, and allocation prioritization.
- Long-term supply chain resilience recommendations: dual-gateway maritime contracting and dynamic multi-port routing software integration.
