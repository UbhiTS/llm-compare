Design an ultra-precision ground Silicon Carbide (SiC) Ceramic and Hard-Anodized Aluminum Semiconductor Lithography / Etch Vacuum Wafer Chuck assembly in millimeters (scaled 1:2 at 150 mm chuck diameter) resting flat on the build plate:

1. **Hard-Anodized Aluminum Base Pedestal & Kinematic Mount Flange**:
   - 164 mm outer diameter base flange (14 mm thick) with 6 perimeter bolt holes and a central 36 mm vacuum & helium backside-cooling manifold bore.
   - Material: dark hard-coat anodized 6061-T6 aluminum.

2. **Precision-Ground Porous Ceramic Vacuum Chuck Platen**:
   - 150 mm diameter upper wafer-clamping table rising 16 mm above the base pedestal, featuring 3 concentric circular vacuum distribution grooves (at radii 24 mm, 46 mm, and 66 mm) separated by precision-lapped annular land rings that hold a silicon wafer flat to sub-micron tolerances.
   - Material: white/ivory sintered alumina / silicon carbide technical ceramic.

3. **Three-Pin Wafer Lift Assembly & Edge Wafer Alignment Pins**:
   - Three vertical precision-honed lift-pin through-bores arranged on a 120-degree bolt circle (at 40 mm radius) housing 3 raised titanium/sapphire wafer lift pins extending above the chuck surface for robotic end-effector wafer handoff, plus 3 perimeter eccentric wafer centering pins.

Make key dimensions adjustable via live sliders (chuck diameter, base thickness, platen height, vacuum ring count, and lift pin extension height).
