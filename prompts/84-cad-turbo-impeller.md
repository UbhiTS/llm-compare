Design a 5-axis CNC-machined Centrifugal Turbocharger Compressor Impeller wheel in millimeters resting flat on the build plate:

1. **Sculpted Conical Inducer-to-Exducer Hub**:
   - Exducer base diameter 112 mm (56 mm radius) with an 8 mm thick backface balance rim sitting on the ground plane, sweeping smoothly inward along a concave parabolic meridian curve up to a 28 mm diameter nose at 54 mm height.
   - Central 16 mm precision shaft bore running vertically through the hub, topped with a 14 mm tall threaded hex spindle locknut at the inducer nose tip.
   - Material: billet Ti-6Al-4V titanium hub with a machined brass spindle locknut.

2. **Swept Full Inducer Blades**:
   - 7 full-height twisted compressor blades (2.2 mm blade thickness) extending from the upper inducer eye down to the outer exducer rim, with a smooth backward helical sweep to compress intake air radially.

3. **Interleaved Splitter Blades**:
   - Shorter splitter blades positioned midway between each pair of main blades in the lower radial diffuser section to prevent flow separation and choking at high RPM.
   - Material: polished 17-4PH stainless steel / titanium aerodynamic surfaces.

Make key dimensions adjustable via live sliders (inducer radius, exducer radius, hub height, main blade count, and shaft bore radius).
