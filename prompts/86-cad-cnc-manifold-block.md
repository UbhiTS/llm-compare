Design a 5-axis CNC-machined 6061-T6 aluminum Hydraulic Directional Valve Manifold Block assembly in millimeters resting flat on the build plate:

1. **Machined Billet Manifold Block**:
   - Rectangular billet body measuring 130 mm long by 86 mm wide by 64 mm tall with 3 mm chamfered/filleted edges and 4 corner M8 vertical mounting through-bores.
   - Internal cross-drilled main pressure gallery (`P`) and return tank gallery (`T`) running longitudinally through the block, intersecting vertical valve feed risers.
   - Material: clear or blue anodized 6061-T6 machined billet aluminum.

2. **Top CETOP / ISO 4401 Solenoid Valve Mounting Pad & Studs**:
   - Precision-ground top mounting pad on the upper face featuring 2 raised brass cartridge relief valve bosses with external threads and hex wrench flats, plus 4 stainless steel socket head cap screws around the valve interface pattern.

3. **Front & Side SAE Split-Flange High-Pressure Ports**:
   - Two heavy-duty flanged high-pressure inlet/outlet port bosses protruding from the front face (28 mm outer diameter, 16 mm inner bore, 14 mm projection) with 4-bolt SAE split-flange mounting collars.
   - Material: brushed stainless steel flange collars and machined brass cartridge plugs.

Make key dimensions adjustable via live sliders (block length, block width, block height, port bore diameter, and cartridge boss height).
