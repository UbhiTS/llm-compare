Design a High-Pressure Die-Cast (HPDC) A380 Aluminum Electric Vehicle (EV) 800V Traction Inverter Housing assembly in millimeters resting flat on the build plate:

1. **Die-Cast Cavity Housing with External Cast Stiffening Ribs**:
   - 160 mm long by 116 mm wide by 46 mm tall thin-walled cast aluminum enclosure (3.5 mm wall thickness, 8 mm cast corner radii) with an exterior perimeter seal flange and 8 M6 bolt bosses around the rim.
   - Material: cast A380 aluminum / titanium metallic finish.

2. **Integrated Pin-Fin Liquid Cooling Cold Plate**:
   - Raised internal thermal dissipation pedestal inside the cavity floor populated with an array of cylindrical/conical cooling pin-fins (7 mm diameter, 16 mm tall) where Silicon Carbide (SiC) power MOSFET modules sit, paired with two externally threaded barbed coolant inlet/outlet nozzles protruding from the left wall.
   - Material: high-conductivity copper thermal pin-fin cold plate and brass coolant fittings.

3. **3-Phase AC Output & HVDC Input Busbar Feedthrough Glands**:
   - Three insulated orange high-voltage AC phase terminal glands (`U`, `V`, `W`) with copper busbar tongues protruding from the front wall.

Make key dimensions adjustable via live sliders (housing length, housing width, housing height, cooling pin rows, and coolant nozzle length).
